# The World Today — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A one-image world front page summing up the news of Tuesday 24 November 2026: 42 dated stories across 8 regions and 12 fields, organised by region under one masthead. The lead story is the collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya — the first UN climate summit since Copenhagen in 2009 to end with no agreement, after nation after nation refused a final text that never mentioned fossil fuels. Around it, the whole day: Ukraine talks stalling in Abu Dhabi and a Gaza strike killing Hamas's internal security chief, Shenzhou-24 carrying the first Pakistani astronaut to Tiangong, hostages freed in Cartagena, a New York jury convicting Huawei, and a UN report counting 47,000 women killed by partners or family in a year. A UTC timeline strip runs under the masthead, and a date-check audit line at the foot records that every story happened or was first reported on Tuesday 24 November 2026, checked against wire reports of that day. The page is composed in code with PIL — crisp rendered text throughout, no photographs.
