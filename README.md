# The World Today — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A one-image newspaper front page summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026. The lead story is the collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya, where more than 90 nations rejected a draft that never mentioned fossil fuels after about 50 hours of overtime. Below it: US-mediated Ukraine peace talks in Abu Dhabi ending with Russia ruling out territorial concessions, Israeli airstrikes in Gaza killing Hamas internal security chief Naeem Abu Nahel and six others, and US states suing Musk's companies over sexualised images made by the Grok chatbot. All four stories were verified from reports published on 24 November 2026. The page is composed in code with PIL (crisp rendered text) around four generated editorial illustrations.
